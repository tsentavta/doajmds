title: How to find DOAJ articles without DOI, ORCID or abstracts description: Ready-made DOAJ API queries to find a journal’s articles without DOI, ORCID, abstracts or keywords, a one-click audit of the whole journal, and how to fix what you find. updated: 2026-10-28 ---

DOAJ does not check metadata quality on ingest: what the publisher sends is what gets published. That is why many journals in DOAJ have articles without a DOI, without author ORCIDs or without an abstract. The easiest way to find them is with DOAJ API queries or a single audit of the whole journal.

Ready-made queries

Replace 1234-5678 with your journal’s ISSN and paste the query into the “Query” field on the “Search” tab.

What to findQuery
Articles without DOIissn:1234-5678 AND NOT _exists_:index.doi
Articles without DOI (alternative)issn:1234-5678 AND NOT bibjson.identifier.type:doi
No author has an ORCIDissn:1234-5678 AND NOT _exists_:bibjson.author.orcid_id
No abstractissn:1234-5678 AND NOT _exists_:bibjson.abstract
No keywordsissn:1234-5678 AND NOT _exists_:bibjson.keywords
No author has an affiliationissn:1234-5678 AND NOT _exists_:bibjson.author.affiliation
No DOI, published 2020–2024issn:1234-5678 AND bibjson.year:[2020 TO 2024] AND NOT _exists_:index.doi

Why NOT _exists_:doi does not work. doi: is a short name that DOAJ understands only when searching by value (doi:10.…). There is no doi field in the index, so _exists_:doi is always false and NOT _exists_:doi returns every article of the journal. To test for a DOI use index.doi.

Audit the whole journal in one click

Queries are good for targeted searches, but they have a limit: you cannot query for “one of the authors has no affiliation” — _exists_ is true if the field is filled for at least one author. The “Export & audit” tab gives the full picture:

  1. Enter the journal ISSN and click “Load” — the app fetches every article, even if there are more than 1000.
  2. Read the summary: no DOI, duplicate DOIs and titles, no abstract, keywords, full-text link, authors, pages, volume or year; “not all authors have affiliation”, “no ORCID at all”, invalid ORCIDs.
  3. Click a problem card — the table shows only those articles, and the export downloads exactly them.

How to fix what you find

  • One or two articles — open the article in the editor (the “Edit” button in search results), add the DOI, ORCID or abstract and save. A publisher API key is required.
  • Many articles — filter them on the “Export & audit” tab, export to CSV, complete the data in Excel and upload it back. How to do that without creating duplicates: Bulk upload articles to DOAJ from Excel and CSV.
  • DOIs are in Crossref but not in DOAJ — paste the journal’s DOI list into “Reconciliation against a DOI list” on the “Export & audit” tab: the app shows which articles are missing from DOAJ and which DOAJ records have no DOI.

Why it matters

DOAJ metadata is freely redistributed to library catalogues, search engines and research discovery services. An article without a DOI is harder to link to its citations, one without ORCIDs to its authors’ profiles, one without an abstract is harder to find. Fix the record in DOAJ and it gets fixed in every service that harvests it.
